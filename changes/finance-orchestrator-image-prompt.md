# Cover image prompt — Finance orchestrator

Same series as the other Ixana AI-programme covers (see `ops-orchestrator-image-prompt.md`, `patent-spend` family): dark editorial illustration, single accent, no text, no logos, no faces.

**Prompt.** A ledger drawn as a single long strip of paper laid flat across a dark table, its rows lit from within; six thinner strips feed into it from the left (payroll, invoices, cards, a web-store receipt, a patent docket, a shipping label), each joining the ledger cleanly at a seam. Above the ledger, three small paper markers stand on their edge — one green, one amber, one grey — each pointing at a row with a fine thread, the thread ending in a folded slip that reads as "evidence". A hand-sized rubber stamp rests beside the strip, un-inked. Warm amber accent on charcoal, shallow depth of field, 3:2, illustration not photograph.

**What it must not show.** Any numbers, currency symbols, a robot, a padlock, a brain, a person.

**When generated.** Save as `public/static/images/project/ixana-finance-orchestrator-cover.png` and change `image` on the `ixana-finance-orchestrator` card in `app/work/constants.ts` (D-20).
