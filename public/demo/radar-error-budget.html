Which term decides whether this works

What this demo is

The error budget from the case study, made adjustable. The proposal, the specified airframe and the cited literature are real; the budget itself is an invented worked illustration, and no flight was ever conducted. It exists to show which term the method actually turns on.

The finding, in one move

Drag the dial from ±0.35 m to the module in our own bill of materials. Every other term in the budget could be made perfect and the method would still miss its own pass mark.

That is the value of failing on paper: the airframe was never built, and the finding cost four literature decompositions rather than a season of flying.

Student research at SRM Kattankulathur, written against an ISRO proposal. The altitude term is modelled as linear in the positioning uncertainty and anchored at the published decomposition point of ±0.35 m; terms combine in quadrature. Every figure here is an invented worked illustration rather than a measurement — no flights were conducted, no radar was calibrated and no hardware was built.
